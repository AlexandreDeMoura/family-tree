HI-FI MOCKUPS · V1
A family tree that reads like an album, not a genealogy database
Four static screens against the Constrained MVP PRD. Desktop web, no interactivity — every state you see is a deliberate specimen, not a live behaviour. Demo family: the Moreaus, Lyon, 1915 → today. 30 people, four generations, one unknown-parent pair, two remarriage branches, one half-sibling, one adoption, six remembered relatives.
DECISION · DECEASED
A warm terracotta ring around the portrait — the only difference from a living node. Same size, same contrast, same colour, plus the year range. No grey, no fade, no symbols. In the card it becomes the words In loving memory.
DECISION · UNKNOWN
Unknown = dashed outline, lowercase monospace, no colour. Known absence = plain sentence (No children). A sage dot means “something left to discover” — never a warning.
DECISION · TYPE & COLOUR
Newsreader for names and headings, Public Sans for interface, monospace only for placeholders. Paper #F4EFE6, ink #2A241D, terracotta #B06C3A, sage #7D8A6A. Photos are striped placeholders — drop real ones in.

§8.2 LAYOUT
Generations sit on fixed rows, oldest at the top. Partners are adjacent; children hang from the junction between their parents. All positions are computed — the organizer can’t drag anything.
§10.4 SECOND PARTNER
Jean-Pierre sits between Anne and Sylvie. The Anne branch drops at one height, the Sylvie branch at another — so Léa reads instantly as a half-sibling of Sophie and Marc without a single label.
§17 / §18 TWO KINDS OF GAP
Under Léa: “No children” — set in plain type. Under Julien: a dashed stub and “children unknown”. Same visual weight as everything else, no red, no triangles.
§4.4 EQUAL PROMINENCE
Six people here are deceased. Cover the year ranges and you can’t tell which — identical card, portrait size and ink. Only the terracotta ring differs.

§15 AGE BUCKETS
All eleven buckets are always listed, in life order. Filled ones carry a count; empty ones read “none yet” in monospace. No dates, no estimation — the organizer’s single choice is the whole model.
WHY DARK HERE
The only dark surface in the product, and only while photos are the subject. Warm brown-black, not grey — the deceased ring becomes a lighter amber so it survives on the dark ground.
OPEN QUESTION
Should the bucket list be ordered oldest-first for deceased relatives, so a card opens on the youngest photo? Worth testing — it’s the emotional payload of §15.

§21.6 SPECIFIC VALIDATION
The message names the rule, both people and both years, and says what to do. Terracotta, not red — this is a fact conflict, not a system failure. Save is visibly inert while it stands.
§18 THREE-WAY CHILDREN
Children is a three-state control: 3 known / No children / Unknown. That segmented control is the only place in the product where known absence is authored, and it’s what the tree renders differently.
§4.2 EFFORT → VALUE
The right rail keeps the viewer’s node in shot the whole time, so the organizer sees what each field buys. “Still to discover” is the completion prompt, worded as curiosity.
